> Placeholder content for the fictional company Puentara.

## Short answer
Freeze your card right away in the Puentara app, then report it as lost or stolen. Our security team reviews every report and sends you a replacement card once the old one is cancelled.

## Steps
1. Open the Puentara app and go to Cards.
2. Tap your card, then tap Freeze card. Freezing stops new payments right away, and you can undo it if you find the card.
3. Tap Report lost or stolen. This cancels the card for good.
4. Check your recent activity. If you see payments you don't recognize, tell us in the same chat.
5. Your new card arrives in 5 to 10 business days in the US, and in 10 to 20 business days in Latin America.

We never ask for your full card number, PIN, CVV or one-time codes.
