## Short answer
We verify every account to protect you and to follow the law. Verification needs a photo of a valid government ID and a selfie, and it usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Steps
1. Go to Settings > Verify identity.
2. Take a photo of the front and back of your ID in good light, with all four corners visible and no glare.
3. Take the selfie without glasses or a hat, facing the camera.
4. If verification is rejected, the app shows the reason. Fix it and try again; you can try up to 3 times.
5. If it is still rejected after 3 tries, a specialist reviews it by hand within 3 business days.
