## Short answer
If a charge is wrong, contact the merchant first: it is often the fastest fix. If that doesn't work, you can open a dispute in the app within 60 days of the charge. We review every dispute and tell you the result, but we can't promise the outcome.

## Steps
1. Go to Activity and tap the charge.
2. Tap Dispute this charge and pick the reason: charged twice, wrong amount, item not received, cancelled subscription, or other.
3. Add what you have: receipts, emails with the merchant, tracking numbers.
4. We reply within 10 business days. Some cases take up to 45 days if the merchant responds.
5. While we review, the charge may show as Under review. If you don't recognize the charge at all, freeze your card first and tell us right away.
