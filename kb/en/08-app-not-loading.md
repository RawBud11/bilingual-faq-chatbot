## Short answer
Most loading problems go away after you update the app, restart your phone or clear the app's cache. Your money is safe while the app is not working.

## Steps
1. Update Puentara from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Close the app completely and restart your phone.
3. On Android, go to Settings > Apps > Puentara > Storage and tap Clear cache. On iPhone, delete the app and install it again. Your account and your money stay the same.
4. Try both Wi-Fi and mobile data. Some networks block the app.
5. If it still fails, reply with your phone model, system version, app version (Settings > About) and a screenshot of the error.
