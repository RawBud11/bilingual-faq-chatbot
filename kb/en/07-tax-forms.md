## Short answer
You can download your tax forms in the app under Settings > Documents > Tax forms. Forms for last year are ready by January 31. We can tell you where to find a form, but we can't give tax advice.

## Steps
1. Go to Settings > Documents > Tax forms.
2. Pick the year and tap Download PDF.
3. If you live in the US, we may ask you to fill out a W-9. If you live outside the US, we ask for a W-8BEN instead. Both are filled out in the app.
4. If your name or address on a form is wrong, update it in Settings > Profile and reply to let us know. We'll issue a corrected form.
5. For questions about what to report or how much tax you owe, check the official tax authority or talk to a tax professional.
