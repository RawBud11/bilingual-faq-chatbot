> Placeholder content for the fictional company Puentara.

## Short answer
Most transfers arrive within 1 to 3 business days. International transfers can take up to 5 business days, and holidays in either country add time. If a transfer fails, the money goes back to your Puentara balance.

## Steps
1. Open Activity and tap the transfer to see its status: Pending, Sent, Completed or Failed.
2. Pending: check the recipient's details (name, account and bank). A small typo can hold a transfer.
3. Sent: the money left Puentara. The receiving bank may take 1 to 2 more business days to post it.
4. Failed: the money goes back to your balance within 3 business days. You can send it again after fixing the details.
5. If a transfer has been pending for more than 5 business days, reply with the transfer reference (it starts with TR-) and we'll trace it.
