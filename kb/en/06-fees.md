## Short answer
Opening an account and receiving money are free. Sending money abroad has a fixed fee plus a small currency exchange margin, and you see the total before you confirm.

## Steps
1. Transfers inside the US: free.
2. International transfers: $2.99 per transfer plus an exchange margin of up to 1%.
3. Card purchases in another currency: 1.5% of the amount.
4. ATM withdrawals: 2 free per month, then $2.50 each. The ATM owner may add its own fee.
5. A replacement card is free once a year, then $5.

To see every fee you paid, go to Activity and filter by Fees.
