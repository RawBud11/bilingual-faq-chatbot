## Short answer
Most login problems are fixed by resetting your password or getting a new login code. If your account is locked after too many attempts, it unlocks on its own after 30 minutes.

## Steps
1. On the login screen, tap Forgot password and follow the link we email you. The link works for 1 hour.
2. If the login code doesn't arrive, check that your phone has signal and tap Resend code. Codes expire after 10 minutes.
3. If you changed your phone number, tap Update phone number on the login screen. We'll verify your identity first.
4. Make sure you have the latest version of the app.
5. If nothing works, reply with the email on your account and the error message you see. Never send us your password or the code.
