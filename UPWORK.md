## Long description

Your customers ask the same things every day: what a transfer costs, why it's still pending, why they can't log in. Half of them write in Spanish. The answers are in your help center, but the team can't cover every hour, and a careless chatbot guesses, promises refunds or asks for a card number.

I spent about three years on the phone with bank customers, so I built a help chat that behaves the way a good agent would. It answers simple questions in the customer's language, from one help article, and shows the source. It won't answer "What's my balance?", because it can't see the account, and it says so. A lost card, fraud or "someone is in my account" goes straight to a person. If a customer types a card number or a code, the chat hides it and reminds them never to share it.

Every handoff lands in an agent inbox with a short summary in English and Spanish and the masked conversation, so nobody has to ask the customer to repeat everything.

It runs free in demo mode, and the repo lists every miss on synthetic sample messages. It's a local demo, not a finished product: for your team, I'd load your own articles, adapt the replies to your process and test it on your questions.

Send me a few of your most common customer questions and a couple of your help articles, and I'll show you how this chat would answer them.
